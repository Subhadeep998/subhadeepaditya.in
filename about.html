About

Science, data, stories & law.

I’m Subhadeep Aditya — a biotechnology postgraduate, healthcare data analyst, consultant, author and independent researcher from Kolkata, now studying law.

The short version

I trained as a biotechnologist, researching genome stability in yeast and quorum sensing in Vibrio cholerae. I then spent three-plus years in healthcare intelligence — at GlobalData, Vatrix Healthcare and GHX — turning medical-device and market data into clear, decision-ready insight.

Along the way I started writing: a practitioner’s handbook on healthcare consulting, a mythic techno-thriller set in modern India, and a book on Alzheimer’s disease. I founded NextLeap Advisory, an educational consulting platform, and ChobiGhor Studios, which explores Kolkata’s heritage through photography and art.

Today I’m pursuing an LL.B. at Techno India University, because the hardest questions in genomics, health data and AI are increasingly legal ones. My focus is healthcare law, intellectual property and AI governance — and I’m open to law-firm internships.
